Who are The OJM Experience

The OJM Experience is a jazz duo formed at USC in Columbia, SC in 2022. We started the band originally to improving our own skills and making tips on the side to help fund ourselves through college. Eventually we started getting gigs and became regular performers at The Soda City Market in Columbia. We play a variety of genres from jazz standards to pop song covers and on most Saturdays you can find us playing at Soda City from 9am - 1pm.

Members

Sam Langdale

Sam Langdale has been playing saxophone for over 10 years. He graduated with his bachelors in Computer Science from the University of South Carolina but music is still his main focus in life.

Isa Mendosa

Isa Mendoza has been playing guitar since 2011. They are the sole rhythm section player for the OJM Experience and are responsible for the bass and guitar parts in the band. ] Isa also graduated from USC with a degree in music and a minor in audio recording.
